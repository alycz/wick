Questions

Wick runs on Solana. Prices come from Deribit options and markets settle on Gemini.

Where do prices come from?

Wick's fair price comes from BTC spot and Deribit's options volatility, with Derive and realized volatility as fallbacks. Gemini's bid and ask are shown next to it.

How is the leverage cap set?

From binary gamma: how sharply a contract's value can swing for a small BTC move. The sharper the swing, the lower the cap.

Who pays winners?

The pool. It fills up from losing trades rather than outside deposits. If the pool runs short, payouts wait in a first-in, first-out queue.
